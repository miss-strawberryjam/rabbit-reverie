<p>I embroidered  the little bear myself too! I've really been wanting to incorporate more cute elements in classic lolita. I hope it's not too cringe :sweat:</p>
<p>I'm just an amateur at sewing so I hope it looks okay~~~ I feel happy and accomplished I did it though!</p>
<p>Actually, getting started on projects is very hard for me. I have lots of ideas and feel inspired a lot... but I always think what I create will be terrible and never actually start. It's not even that I actively think it and can try to stop myself. I just... feel it. It sucks. This is something I need to work on changing. I hope you'll see more handmade things from me in the future!</p>
<p>Brown is a new color for me in lolita. I used to dislike it so much because I feel like it doesn't go well with my skin color. But now I just don't care... I wonder if those Korean color specialists are screaming now :sweat:</p>

<p>I need to be thankful for my friend Michelle.. she booked the table for the swap meet and shared it with me! So I got to be a vendor and sell things I do not wear anymore from my wardrobe.</p>

<img src="/assets/other_pics/swap_meet/20250719_123829.jpg" class="img-rounded img-responsive center-block" style="max-height: 55%; max-width: 55%;"><br>
<p>Here's a picture of me at the swap meet. In hindsight, our table was heckin' messy hahahaha, but oh well... </p>
<p>I was able to sell a number of things! There often are not many classic lolitas (it's the least popular substyle of lolita probably) so I'm kind of surprised I was able to sell things! Maybe it's finally picking up in popularity ^^ I feel like dresses from Victorian Maiden are often slow to sell, but I was able to sell a couple! Surprisingly I got no bites on my Juliette et Justine dresses. Victorian Maiden tends to be more muted in their designs (like small florals like rococo bouquet) where as JetJ does the loud painting dresses. I thought loud dresses would appeal to lolitas more since it's more attention grabbing!</p>

<p>I'm kind of glad subtle is getting more popular because it's the style that captured my heart many years ago and made me fall in love with lolita. I like simple, put together looks that don't need to speak loudly to look beautiful~~ I used to feel so alone at meet ups because I was the only one wearing a subtle and probably "boring" coordinate.</p>
