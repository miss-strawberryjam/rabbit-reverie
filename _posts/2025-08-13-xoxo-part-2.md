<p>I hope it's not too inappropriate to share some of my deepest feelings here.</p>
<p>Life has been difficult for me.</p>
<p>I lost my friend group.</p>
<p>I lost my marriage of 10+ years and am having to fight for custody.</p>
<p>It felt like I had no one. I was never close with my family either.</p>
<p>It's so scary when it feels like no one loves you.. when you come home and it's just you... </p>
<p>Things are getting better now. I'm making new friends, I have a date I really like.</p>
<p>I'm accumulating a list of things people have said to me that prove I am not a bad person.</p>
<p>At the same time, I'm trying to remember my worth is not determined by others.</p>
<p>Instead of thinking of these things, I'm trying to channel my energy to more positive endeavors like crafting.</p>
<p>I carry a lot of pain with me... I hope one day I can count on a consistent source of being seen.</p>
<p>I hide a lot of my pain now to not burden others. I hope someone can accept me for who I am.</p>
<p>I hope it'll be okay.</p>
